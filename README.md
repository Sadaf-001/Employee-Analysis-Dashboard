# Employee Attrition Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI report that analyzes employee attrition to help HR spot areas of concern and plan retention strategies.

## Dashboard pages

1. **Employee analysis.** Headcount distribution by department, job role, years at company, education, work-life balance and overtime. Filters for age, gender, job satisfaction and marital status.
2. **Employee correlations.** KPI cards for total attrition, attrition rate, active employees, overall employees and average age. Attrition rate broken down by gender, business travel, distance from home, environment satisfaction, job satisfaction and job level. Filters for department, marital status, monthly income, education and years in current role.
3. **Insights and suggestions.** Findings and recommendations (below).

## Key findings

- Employees who live farther from work and travel frequently for business have higher attrition.
- Attrition is higher among male employees (17.01%) than female employees (14.80%).

## Recommendations from the report

- Offer flexible work arrangements (telecommuting, flexible hours) to frequent travelers, use virtual meetings to reduce travel, and provide relocation assistance for employees who live far away.
- Run exit interviews with departing employees to find underlying issues, and recognize and reward employee contributions.

## Data

Table: `HR-Employee-Attrition` (IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition sample dataset).
